An AI agent carries out a task end to end inside your tools, under clear control rules. Alawen provides specialists who have already put such agents into production.

  • multi-agent orchestration;
  • retrieval-augmented generation (RAG);
  • Model Context Protocol (MCP), to connect agents to your tools;
  • agent evaluation and red teaming;
  • model fine-tuning.

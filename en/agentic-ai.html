One AI agent in production on a single use case, measured before going further

Alawen runs fixed-price agentic AI projects for banking and finance. Every project starts with a free audit, then a 6 to 8 week pilot measured against thresholds set in advance.

Who it is for

The offer is for asset managers, fintechs, payment and electronic money institutions, crypto-asset service providers (CASPs), brokers and insurers.

It is for the business head who carries the cost of the process, with sign-off from the IT department or the CISO.

Projects run remotely, in French or in English.

The method

Any use case the audit qualifies is eligible. The examples below are cases already built by the specialists Alawen works with. They do not set a limit.

Each project is delivered by agentic AI specialists selected by Alawen.

How it works

1. Free audit

A workshop with your team, then a one-hour debrief. The audit is run by an agentic AI specialist.

You leave with:

  • three use cases ranked by quantified gain;
  • one recommended use case;
  • the pilot page for that use case, which sets, before the first run, what the agent does, what it never does, the success thresholds and the decision planned for each result.

The specialist who runs the audit receives no bonus if a pilot is signed.

2. Measured pilot

The pilot lasts 6 to 8 weeks, on the single use case chosen at the audit. It is a fixed price, quoted after the audit.

The agent works in production on a restricted scope. It is measured against the thresholds written on the pilot page.

At the end, the decision is already on paper: extend, fix or stop.

3. Delivered or subscription

After the pilot, you choose what comes next.

Delivered. The agent is delivered and handed over to you. For the company that wants to own the tool. Hosting, maintenance and on-demand days remain available.

Subscription. Alawen keeps the agent in hand: design, maintenance and adjustments to real use included. An agent drifts when real use moves away from the initial scope, and the subscription is there to correct it as it goes.

Use case examples

AI-augmented CISO

An outsourced, part-time shared CISO, backed by an AI CISO platform: DORA, PSD2 and PSD3, ISO 27001, NIS2, the register of ICT third-party providers required by DORA, reports for management. Supervised code audits and penetration tests.

This use case follows its own path. After the audit, a baseline review goes through compliance regime by regime and audits the code. It leads to a remediation plan, then to an ongoing CISO service.

Compliance and operations of a regulated institution

AML/CFT and KYC files are prepared by AI, the analyst decides. Ledgers are reconciled, blockchain included. Each function has a view of what is waiting for it today.

Technical due diligence before an acquisition

Before signing, a full review of the code, security and compliance of the target regulated platform. Every finding is sourced, independently cross-checked, and leads to a costed remediation plan. For investment funds and M&A transactions.

Other examples

  • Routing requests between support and product teams.
  • Conversational banking assistant.
  • Data cross-analysis: segmentation, churn, AML/CFT alerts.
